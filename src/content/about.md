Austin Falk is an actor, creator, and M&A advisor. He is known for recurring roles on School Spirits (Paramount+) and 2 Broke Girls (CBS). Falk is a creator on Instagram, TikTok, and X, sharing his experiences with his followers.

## Career

Raised in Arizona, Falk started acting at a young age in school and church productions. He dove into the craft professionally while attending university in Southern California. Falk's career spans film and television, along with continued exploration in the world of finance and technology. In 2026 he joined Forge Origination as a buy-side M&A advisor. Falk is focused on leaving behind a legacy of passion and devotion to the family, friends, and co-workers he spends time with.

## Entertainment

Falk is a member of SAG-AFTRA and UBCP/ACTRA and has trained in drama, comedy, and improvisation. He has worked with directors such as Wally Pfister, Fred Savage, and Max Winkler.

- _Killer in Suburbia_ (A+E, 2020)

## Finance

Falk is a buy-side M&A advisor at Forge Origination. He works with private equity and business owners to find opportunities for growth, while bolstering their respective foundations.

## Technology

Falk builds tools for consumers and businesses across the web, mobile, and desktop environments. A macOS user from the time he could reach the keyboard, and a recent convert to Omarchy, he continues to explore the bleeding edge of what modern technology has to offer.

## Fitness

Falk trains regularly and maintains a precise diet. Long walks and other lindy pursuits fill a lifestyle geared around enjoying the longevity journey.

## Education

Falk graduated magna cum laude at the age of 20 with a bachelor's degree from the Crowell School of Business at Biola University in Southern California.
