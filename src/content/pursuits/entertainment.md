Falk has worked in film and television for over a decade as a SAG-AFTRA and UBCP/ACTRA member, with recurring roles on Paramount+'s School Spirits and CBS's 2 Broke Girls. A seeker of truth and connection, he was drawn to acting from a young age and shares his experiences with more than 50,000 followers across Instagram, TikTok, and X.
